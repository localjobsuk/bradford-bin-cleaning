Residential Bin Cleaning – FAQs

How often should household bins be cleaned?

Most households choose 4-weekly cleaning, although higher frequency options are available.

Do you clean both general waste and recycling bins?

Yes, we clean general waste, recycling and garden waste bins.

Do I need to be home during service?

No, as long as the bin is accessible following collection.

What if I miss a bin collection day?

We can reschedule within the service cycle where possible.

Do you cover low-density rural areas?

Yes, pricing may vary depending on route allocation and access.
